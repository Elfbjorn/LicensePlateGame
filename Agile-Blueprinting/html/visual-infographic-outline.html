Leadership Infographic

Purpose:

Introduce the core Agile Blueprinting message to IT leadership.

Core message:

Waterfall's clarity, agile's cadence, AI's compression.

Suggested structure:

  • Title: Agile Blueprinting
  • Center triad:
    • Waterfall clarity: known target, complete context
    • Agile cadence: short feedback loops, time-boxed review
    • AI compression: faster build, test, and evidence generation
  • Bottom outcome:
    • Faster delivery without compressing accountability

Design direction:

  • 16:9 executive slide / infographic
  • three-column or triangular composition
  • subtle process line converging into governed delivery
  • minimal text, high legibility
  • conservative government briefing feel
