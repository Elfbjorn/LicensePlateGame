Use this template to define a complete capability before AI-assisted delivery begins. The goal is not heavyweight documentation. The goal is clear intent, known deliverables, short feedback loops, and agent-ready execution.

Success Criteria

Definition of Ready Checklist

  • [ ] Product Manager or Business Analyst owns the PRD.
  • [ ] Customer or customer proxy has been interviewed.
  • [ ] Customer need and desired outcome are clear.
  • [ ] End-to-end workflow has been walked through.
  • [ ] Low-fi mockups, wireframes, workflow diagrams, or equivalent aids have been reviewed if needed.
  • [ ] Major contingencies, alternate flows, and exceptions have been considered.
  • [ ] Data, rules, validations, integrations, and permissions are documented or explicitly marked as assumptions.
  • [ ] Acceptance criteria are specific enough for QA and customer validation.
  • [ ] Out-of-scope items are explicit.
  • [ ] Product / BA owner signs off that this PRD represents the intended deliverable.
  • [ ] Customer or customer proxy accepts the blueprint direction.

Amendment Policy
